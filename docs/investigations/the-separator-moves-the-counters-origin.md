# The sync separator moves the counter's origin

A source whose hsync is regenerated by the TV5725's sync separator — composite
sync and sync on green both, `SP_SOG_MODE` 1 — reaches the input formatter's
line counter late. Its video therefore lands **earlier in the counter** than its
published raster states, and a capture window placed as though it had its own
hsync line cuts the left of the picture and leaves black at the right.

`VideoSourceLine::SeparatorOriginPerThousand` was 70, and is gone.

**THE FRACTION IS REFUTED AND THE FORM IS SETTLED: the delay is the source's
measured sync width less 66 ADC samples.** Six published modes, read as the
difference between the two sync types, give that to within 1.5 samples where a
fraction of the line leaves a 66-unit residual. The two readings below were
taken against the published raster on one sync type, which carries each mode's
own systematic, and while `SP_RT_HS_SP` was 61 samples wrong — so they do not
stand as measurements of the delay. 320x256@50 re-measures at 87.7 samples and
640x480@60 at 103.2.
[the-separator-lead-is-not-a-fraction-of-the-line.md](the-separator-lead-is-not-a-fraction-of-the-line.md)

## A count and a time are both refuted

Measured on the RISC PC on `vga`, `PATTERN CARD`, reading the card's one-pixel
green frame off the emitted frame with `hdmi_capture.py`. The machine takes its
sync type from CMOS and ModeServ sets it, so `SYNC 0` and `SYNC 1` are one
command apart on one cable and one raster, with nothing else moving.

| mode | scan | counter | `PLLAD_MD` | separate − composite | fraction |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 320x256@50 | doubled | 1100 | 2200 | **76.5** | **6.96%** |
| 640x480@60 | undoubled | 1446 | 1446 | **103, 109** | **7.1%, 7.5%** |

Each figure is the whole picture's displacement, taken from the green frame's
own edges and converted at `1920 / eh` output pixels per capture unit — a
calibration the span checks independently: at 640x480@60 the green frame spans
1157.5 units against the 1157.6 DMT states for 640 active pixels of 800.

**A constant count is refuted.** Fitted to 640x480@60 it predicts 50 units at
the bench mode against 76.5 measured — 40% apart, where the bench-mode figure
repeats to the pixel across two acquisitions.

**A constant time is refuted** by the handover's 800x600@60 point: 2.2 µs would
read 120 units there and it reads about 100.

A fraction of the line fits these two and nothing wider: it is the same thing as
a constant phase, and the sync processor counting in ADC clocks makes that the
natural shape. A third mode at the same line rate breaks it, and sync width is
what that mode varies.

## The floor does NOT come down with it

Only the video travels along the counter. What the capture path writes at the
head stays where the counter puts it, so `CaptureWindow::firstCapture()` is
`syncUnits + headBlanking` on both arrangements.

Measured at 320x256@50 with automation frozen and `IF_HB_SP2` forced to the same
unit on each sync type, counting columns of saturated green at the head of the
emitted line:

| `IF_HB_SP2` | 52 | 60 | 70 | 80 | 85 | 88 | 90 | 100 | 110 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| composite | 74 | 58 | 38 | 17 | — | **0** | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| separate | — | — | **0** | 0 | 0 | — | 0 | 0 | 0 |

So the blanking reaches unit 88 on the separator's path and nothing measurable
on separate sync, at the same divider, the same mode and the same register. A
floor discounted by the lead opens at 52 and takes 36 units of it into the
picture as a green band down the left of the screen.

**What follows is that a doubled line cannot spend the whole lead.** The floor
is 101 at this mode, so the window reaches it after 28 of the 77 units and the
rest of that part of the line is not capturable at all. An undoubled line has no
head blanking and spends more of it before the pulse stops it.

**AND ON A WIDE-SYNC MODE THE VIDEO ARRIVES BELOW THE FLOOR.** At 640x480@60 on
composite sync the source's active video reaches counter unit 150 against a floor
of 168, so the card's left green line is absent from the emitted frame at any
framing while the right one is there with blanking beyond it. The floor is safe
at this mode and not at that one.

**Why the blanking is there on one arrangement and not the other is not known.**
It is not the lead moving a fixed feature -- that would put it EARLIER in the
counter on the separator's path, and it reads later.

## The vertical carries the same delay, as a count of lines

`VideoSourceLine::SeparatorFrameLeadHalfLines` is 35, and the counter counts
half-lines with the line doubler in, so the lead doubles with the scan.

Measured as the displacement of the card's bottom green line between the two
sync types, converted at `1024 / VDS_VSCALE` output rows to the capture unit:

| mode | frame counter | output rows | capture units | source lines |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 640x480@60 | 525 | 34 | 15.2 | **15.2** |
| 320x256@50 | 624 | 61 | 33.1 | **16.6** |

**A fraction is refuted**: 2.9% of the frame against 5.3%, where the line count
agrees to 9%. The horizontal is the other way round, and the two need not agree
— one is a delay through a PLL locked to the line, the other a delay through
whatever counts lines.

Solved at 640x480@60 the emitted frame's black margins are top 5 / bottom 5 on
composite against top 0..3 / bottom 5..6 on separate, where composite sat 34
rows up before.

### THE REFERENCE EDGE MOVES THIS ORIGIN, SO THE LEAD BELONGS TO AN EDGE

The two readings above were taken while the separator referenced the TRAILING
edge of the pulse. Composite sync references the LEADING edge now, and the frame
counter's origin moves with it -- so a lead measured on one edge frames nothing
on the other, which is how 16 lines came to leave the bench mode with its top
border off the emitted frame and seven rows of the source's own blanking at the
bottom.

Measured frozen at 320x256@50 into 1080p, writing `SP_CS_P_SWAP` and
`SP_HS2PLL_INV_REG` by hand with one vertical capture window in force, and
reading the row profile with the picture lifted so the source's blanking reads
grey where ours reads black:

| reference | top of the frame | bottom of the frame |
|---|---|---|
| leading, 1/1 | `0 177 234 234 234` -- inside the picture | `128 102 51 30 30 30 30 30 30 0` |
| trailing, 0/0 | `0 30 30 30 30 56 102 119` -- blanking, then the border | `216 216 210 157 119 119 0` |

Four units apart, repeated and reproduced across three writes. `SP_CS_P_SWAP`
alone carries about half of it, so both bits move the origin.

**THE POST COAST IS NOT IT.** The same four edges read margins `1|7` and
wholeness `0.10|0.68` at a post coast of 2, 4 and 6, with `IF_VB_ST` and
`IF_VB_SP` identical at each.

### 17.5 LINES IS WHAT FRAMES BOTH SCAN MODES

Measured by panning the vertical capture window a unit at a time -- both ends by
the same delta, so the width, the scale and the parity are untouched -- and
judging the card's border with `card_edges.judge()`. The delta is from the
window a lead of 16 lines solved, and the figures are the two edges' wholeness:

| mode | scan | -1 | -2 | -3 | -4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 320x256@50 | doubled | 0.60/0.89 | 0.83/0.84 | **0.86/0.86** | 0.89/0.61 |
| 800x600@60 | undoubled | 0.52/0.91 | **0.87/0.83** | off the frame | off the frame |

The doubled mode's optimum sits between -2 and -3 and the undoubled one's at
-2 -- the same count of UNITS on both scan modes, and so 1.25 source lines
against 2. As a total lead that is 44.5 units doubled and 25 undoubled, and one
count of lines gives both to within half a unit: **17.5**.

A whole count cannot. 17 lands the doubled mode and leaves the undoubled one a
unit short at 0.52 of its border; 18 lands the undoubled one and costs the
doubled mode most of its far border, at 0.61. So the lead is held in half lines
and rounds up on an undoubled source, where the counter has no half to land on.

**IT IS THE RISC PC ON COMPOSITE THAT MEASURES THIS, AND NOTHING ELSE HERE
CAN.** The Wii's sync on green takes the leading edge too, but its 480p carries
fourteen rows of its own black at the top of the emitted frame and thirteen at
the bottom, so a shift of a line or two is invisible there -- the capture has
525 units for 480 active lines and the slack absorbs it.


## What it is not

**`SP_RT_HS_SP` is inert.** The firmware writes it at 93% of `PLLAD_MD`, so
`1 − SP_RT_HS_SP / PLLAD_MD` is 0.0700 — the measured fraction to two figures,
which is a coincidence and not a mechanism. Written at 1946, 2146, 1345 and 700,
with and without `SFTRST_SYNC_RSTZ` pulsed, the picture does not move at all.

**The window registers no longer differ between the two arrangements**, so this
is not the polarity error
[the-composite-capture-window-sits-between-two-wrong-values.md](the-composite-capture-window-sits-between-two-wrong-values.md)
records. That one was `HsyncPulse` carrying the measured polarity into
`syncAtHead`, a whole pulse of displacement, and it went with the pulse's
polarity. What is left is this, and it is about half a pulse — which is where
that page's forced-window experiment put the truth.

Nothing on the sync processor reaches it. Measured inert: `SP_H_PULSE_IGNOR`,
`SP_HS_EP_DLY_SEL`, `SP_RT_HS_ST`, `SP_RT_HS_SP`, `SP_RT_VS_ST`, `SP_CS_HS_ST`,
`SP_NO_COAST_REG`, `SP_H_CST_SP`, `SP_PRE_COAST`, `SP_POST_COAST`, `SP_HS_REG`,
`SP_SYNC_WIDTH_DTHD`, `SP_T_DLT_REG`, `SP_L_DLT_REG`, `SP_HT_DIFF_REG`,
`SP_H_TOTAL_EQ_THD`, `SP_DIS_SUB_COAST`, `SP_HS2PLL_INV_REG`. Wrong direction or
destructive: `SP_HD_MODE`, `SP_CS_P_SWAP`, `SP_HS_PROC_INV_REG`,
`SP_CS_INV_REG`, `SP_SOG_P_INV`. `SP_CS_HS_SP` moves the picture one for one and
sits at its floor of 0 pushing the wrong way; `SP_HS_LOOP_SEL` accounts for 7
units of it.

**The source is not shifting the edge.** VIDC20 makes composite sync by XNORing
H and V, so the horizontal edge the separator sees is the one the separate-sync
arrangement puts on its own pin. The delay is the separator's.

## How to measure it again

`PATTERN CARD`'s green frame marks the source's outermost pixels, so a
displacement is a number rather than a judgement. Take the **outermost** green
above a threshold, requiring a run most of the picture's height — the card
carries green colour blocks in its middle, and `argmax` finds one of those
instead.

At a default framing on a published mode both green edges are flush with the
raster and saturate, so force a 100% framing to read them; at 320x256@50 the
default framing already shows both.

Two acquisitions of the same state can differ by a displacement nobody wrote
— 20 output pixels measured here — so take the two sync types as a pair with no
mode change between them, and repeat the pair.

**A `SYNC` command repaints the card as `PM5544`.** Send `PATTERN CARD` after
every one, or the green measured is the colour bar in the middle of a different
card.

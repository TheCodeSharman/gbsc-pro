# The separator's lead is the sync width less a count of ADC samples

A source whose hsync is regenerated by the TV5725's sync separator — composite
sync and sync on green both, `SP_SOG_MODE` 1 — reaches the input formatter's
line counter late, so its video lands earlier in the counter than its published
raster states. `VideoSourceLine::SeparatorPulseEndLeadSamples` is 66 and the
delay is

```
delay = measured sync width − 66      both in ADC samples
```

clamped at zero, converted to counter units at two samples to the unit where
the line is doubled.

`SeparatorOriginPerThousand` was 70 — a fraction of the LINE, fitted to one mode
while `SP_RT_HS_SP` was 61 samples wrong, and the contamination
[`the-retime-stop-is-the-counters-origin.md`](the-retime-stop-is-the-counters-origin.md)
predicted.

## What is measured

RISC PC on `vga`, `PATTERN CARD`, `RetroScaler-Acorn.mdf`, output 1920x1080,
read off the USB HDMI capture. The card's one-pixel green frame marks the
source's outermost active pixel.

**The delay is the DIFFERENCE between the two sync types on one mode.** A
position read against a published raster carries that mode's own systematic —
whether the machine emits the raster the file states, where the sink placed its
window for that raster, where the counter's own origin sits — and only the
difference is the separator:

| mode | counter | `HLOW` | composite | separate | **delay** | `HLOW` − delay |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1024x768@60 | 1440 | 137 | 145.2 | **75.3** | 69.9 | **67.1** |
| 720x480@60 | 1442 | 104 | 31.1 | −7.5 | 38.6 | **65.4** |
| 800x600@56 | 1536 | 107 | 53.5 | 11.7 | 41.8 | **65.2** |
| 640x480@60 | 1444 | 169 | 108.6 | 5.4 | 103.2 | **65.8** |
| 800x600@60 | 1438 | 173 | 104.6 | −3.4 | 108.0 | **65.0** |
| 320x256@50, doubled | 1100 | 154 | 34.2 | −9.7 | 87.7 | **66.3** |

Every figure is counter units except `HLOW` and the last column, which are ADC
samples. A free fit on the measured width returns

```
delay = 1.000 × HLOW − 65.7      rms 0.77 samples, worst residual 1.44
```

and the slope is fitted rather than imposed.

## Every other shape is refuted

Residuals over the same six modes:

| shape | rms | worst |
|---|---|---|
| **the sync width less 66 samples** | **0.8** | **1.4** |
| a fraction of the line (the old 70 per mille) | 43 | 66 |
| the best single fraction of the line | 30 | 36 |
| a constant count of units | 29 | 36 |
| the sync width itself | 69 | 76 |
| the best fraction of the sync width | 15 | 23 |
| a constant time | — | 0.9 µs |
| affine in the sync width, in counter units | 3.7 | 6.9 |

**A fraction of the line dies on duty alone**: three modes near a 7% sync duty
read 2.16%, 3.48% and 3.11% of the line.

**The units are ADC samples, and only the doubled mode says so.** At 320x256@50
a constant in counter units predicts a separate-sync reading of 22.9 against
−9.7 measured, where one in samples predicts −10.0.

**66 is not the retime origin.** `SP_RT_HS_SP` subtracts the measured width and
adds `SyncProcessor::RetimeOriginSamples` on BOTH sync types, so the retime
cancels in the difference. Fitting 63 instead leaves rms 2.8 with every residual
the same sign.

## What it fixes

The Wii at 480p on `ypbpr`, sync on green, the engine's own solve into 1920x1080,
read off the capture:

| | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| applied lead | 101 units | 40 units |
| capture window | picture 107..1321 | `IF_HB_SP2` 164..1380 |
| black at the left of the emitted frame | **134 px** | **20 px** |
| picture cut at the right | 44 px | none |

Its content spans capture units 191.5..1348.7, so the window now contains it
with 27 units of blanking at the head and 31 at the tail.

The RISC PC at 320x256@50 opens its capture at 191 on composite against 236 on
separate — a difference of 45 units, which is `(156 − 66) / 2` on a doubled
counter.

## How to measure it again

**Take both sync types on each mode and difference them.** `SYNC 0` and `SYNC 1`
are one ModeServ command apart on one cable and one raster. Reading a single sync
type against the published raster is what produced the earlier two-point
reading, and the two modes it used happened to share an output raster, which hid
the systematic.

**Force the sync-type re-probe and check it took.** `establishSyncType()` keys on
the INPUT selection, so a `SYNC` command leaves the engine on the path it held.
`/sc?~` forgets it; read `SP_SOG_MODE` back before measuring. A freeze across the
change blocks the re-probe outright.
[`a-sync-type-change-arms-no-probe.md`](a-sync-type-change-arms-no-probe.md)

**Gate on the divider, not on `acquired`.** `STATUS_SYNC_PROC_HTOTAL` against
`PLLAD_MD` is the comparison with a right answer.

**A latched separate-sync path answers to `/restart` and not to `/sc?~`.** After
a mode the separator never reads, the engine sits at `SP_SOG_MODE` 0 on a
composite source with `SP_H_PULSE_IGNOR` 255, `SP_RT_HS_SP` at the bare origin
and no vertical count. Three `/sc?~` cycles left it there; `/restart` cleared it
in 28 s, repeatably.

### Three traps, each of which cost a reading

- **The green frame needs about 1.4 capture units per source pixel.** Below that
  the one-pixel line falls between samples, which is where
  `dividerCeilingForOutput()` puts 1024x768@60 at 1.07, 800x600@72 at 1.23 and
  1280x480@73 at 0.87. 1024x768@60 nevertheless resolved; the other two did not.
- **A scale the tail walk cannot measure invalidates the frame.** Where full
  framing does not reach the end of the line the display window runs to the
  frame's edge, the tail has no black run to walk, and the fit returns a
  negative slope with residuals in the hundreds. Gate on it: both frames that
  survived to a plausible-looking lead had it.
- **The bright artefact at the blanking edge reads as a green line.** At
  320x256@50 the detector found three groups and the first sat on the anchor
  column. Skip a group that starts within a few columns of it, or the left edge
  is measured from the artefact.

**The head black run is NOT truncated, and that was tested rather than
assumed.** Two modes read an anchor of one and two columns, which a window
starting off the left of the frame would also give. Walking `VDS_DIS_HB_SP`
blanks more of the head without moving the picture, so the run's end is linear
in it; extrapolating back to the value in force gives the window's true start.
On 800x600@56 the head walk's slope matched the tail walk's to 0.1%, the green
line held still to 0.0 px, and the extrapolated anchor differed from the read
one by **0.1 capture units**. The readings stand.

## The form is applied as stated, and the doubled mode has left it

Re-measured 2026-10-18 against the framing matrix at 1080p, the framing reset
and 16:9 declared. **The lead reaches the capture window exactly as the form
says**, so nothing has regressed in how it is applied:

| mode | `HLOW` sep / csync | `IF_HB_ST2` sep / csync | applied | `HLOW` - 66 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 320x256@50, doubled | 156 / 153 | 926 / 884 | 42 units = **84 samples** | 87 |
| 640x480@60 | 147 / 148 | 1248 / 1170 | **78 samples** | 82 |
| 800x600@60 | 164 / 161 | 1293 / 1202 | **91 samples** | 95 |

Each is about four samples under `HLOW - 66`, and that is not the rounding:
`hsyncPulseSamples()` takes `SyncProcessor::InvertedPulseWidthSamples` off a
reading the path widened, which a separated one always is, so the lead is
computed from a pulse five samples narrower than the register holds. Scored
against what `forDuty()` computes from that pulse, four of the five modes below
apply it to the unit.

**What has moved is the residual on the emitted frame.** Differencing the two
sync types' black margin at the SAME edge, which is the only form of the
comparison that measures one feature:

| mode | residual after the lead | this page's fit |
|---|---|---|
| 640x480@60 | **+1.06 samples** | 65.8 |
| 800x600@60 | **+1.06 samples** | 65.0 |
| 320x256@50, doubled | **+15.7 samples** | 66.3 |

So the two published modes are right to a sample and the doubled one
over-compensates by about eight counter units -- it wants a constant near **82**
rather than 66, where this page's own six-mode fit put it at 66.3.

## The doubler is what leaves it, and the raster's authority is not

Measured 2026-10-18 over five modes that acquire on both sync types, the framing
reset and 16:9 declared, each judged at the frame it emits.
`separator_lead.py` is the probe: it reads the window's displacement between the
two sync types and the residual the emitted frame still shows at ONE edge.

| mode | lines | counter unit | raster | duty | lead applied / computed | residual, source px | in samples |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 320x256@50 | 312 | 2 samples | Acorn | 0.070 | 42 / 42 | **+3.33** | +14.3 |
| 640x200@60 | 262 | 2 samples | Acorn | 0.071 | 42 / 42 | **+3.33** | +7.2 |
| 320x256@70 | 449 | 1 sample | none | 0.089 | 40 / 46 | **-1.50** | -5.3 |
| 640x480@60 | 525 | 1 sample | DMT | 0.116 | 78 / 78 | **-0.00, +0.67** | 0.0, +1.1 |
| 800x600@60 | 628 | 1 sample | DMT | 0.122 | 91 / 91 | **+0.83** | +1.1 |

Positive is the composite picture sitting further into the frame than the
separate one, which is the lead over-compensating. 640x480@60 was taken twice
and the pair bounds the instrument at about two thirds of a source pixel: one
output column of margin is 0.33 pixels there.

**THE SOURCE'S OWN PIXEL IS THE UNIT THAT MAKES THE MODES COMPARABLE.** An ADC
sample is a different length on every mode -- 4.30 samples to the pixel on
320x256@50 against 1.59 on 640x480@60 -- so a residual in samples cannot be read
across the table without the divider beside it. The two doubled modes differ by
a factor of two in samples and agree to 0.00 of a pixel.

**THE DOUBLER SPLITS THE TABLE AND NOTHING ELSE IN IT DOES.** Both modes on a
doubled counter over-compensate; all three on an undoubled one land within a few
samples of flush.

**640x200@60 IS WHAT RETIRES THE RASTER AS A CANDIDATE.** It is a published
Acorn mode at 0.071 duty -- the same mode file and the same sync width as
320x256@50, differing in frame and field rate -- and it over-compensates by 7.2.
320x256@70 matches no raster at all, carries the narrow duty, and does not. So
neither the authority that states the raster nor the sync width separates the
two groups.

**320x256@70 IS AN UNUSUAL MODE AND IS WEIGHTED AS ONE.** It exists to put 256
lines on a VGA monitor, so it runs them at a 31.5 kHz line rate inside a
449-line frame -- letterboxed, 106 lines of back porch and 85 of front. That is
vertical: across the line it is an ordinary 400 pixels with 12-pixel borders,
which is the axis this measures, and its vertical reading is 200 columns of the
source's own letterbox at each end. It is also the one mode whose applied lead
misses what `forDuty()` computes, by 6 units, with its window 34 units clear of
the floor.

**640x352@60 CANNOT MEASURE THE LEAD, AND THE REASON IS THE FLOOR.** Its 364
lines are doubled -- the doubler is selected on the line COUNT, under
`InputFormatter::DoubleBelowLines`, so at 21.9 kHz it is doubled where
`/geometry`'s `lowLineRate` says it is not -- which gives a 1100-unit counter
with 22 units of head blanking past a 106-unit pulse. The composite window
opens at 129 on a floor of 131 against a computed lead of 73 units, so what the
two sync types differ by is the floor and not the lead. Measured twice, 48 units
both times.

**WHICH REOPENS THE UNITS.** This page's conclusion that the constant is a count
of ADC samples rather than counter units rests on the doubled mode alone, and
the doubled modes are the two that disagree. Nor is one constant enough for
them: zeroing the residual wants 80 samples on 320x256@50 and 73 on 640x200@60,
against 67 on the two DMT modes.

**A CONSTANT 3.33 SOURCE PIXELS IS WHAT THE TWO DOUBLED MODES READ, AND TWO
POINTS CANNOT DISCONFIRM IT.** They agree to better than a hundredth of a pixel
across frames of 312 and 262 lines, field rates of 50 and 60, and magnifications
of 2.43 and 2.02 -- and that is one parameter fitted to two readings, which a
refuted two-term fit on eleven readings is the standing warning about.
[moving-write-origin.md](moving-write-origin.md)

What would test it is a third doubled mode whose active count is a DIFFERENT
share of its line: both modes here paint 0.625 of theirs, so a constant share of
the active pixel and one that follows the source's line pixels predict the same
figure for both. **640x240@60 is that mode** -- 262 lines, the other polarity
pair, 640 pixels of an 858-pixel line -- and it predicts 10 output columns on the
first reading against 12 on the second.

**ITS DEFAULT FRAMING CROPS ALL FOUR EDGES, so it needs a framing applied to
both legs before it can be read.** Measured on both sync types: no green on any
edge, the capture taking 691 of 1100 counter units where the source paints 640 of
858 pixels. The lead itself is applied there exactly as `forDuty()` computes it,
44 units against 44, which is the fourth mode to agree.

640x512@50 is still the undoubled 50 Hz case and its composite leg still does
not acquire.

**A DIFFERENCE TAKEN BETWEEN TWO DIFFERENT EDGES IS NOT A DISPLACEMENT.** Where
one sync type leaves a margin at the near edge and the other runs off it, the
two readings measure different features and differencing them gives a number
with no meaning. 240x352@70 is in that state and carries no residual here for
that reason.

## What is not settled

**Why 66.** It is a count of ADC samples rather than a time, which suits a
digital pulse-width discriminator — `SP_H_PULSE_IGNOR` and `SP_DLT_REG` are
clocked by the ADC clock — but nothing derives the figure.

**A source whose sync is narrower than 66 samples.** The form clamps to zero
there and no measurement reaches it: every CEA HD raster qualifies, and
1280x720@60 refuses the composite path on this machine, three attempts, the
engine landing on `SP_SOG_MODE` 0 with no vertical count.

**Whether the sliced and logic-level front ends differ.** Sync on green reaches
the separator through the SOG comparator and composite sync on `vga` through the
dedicated HSync pin. The Wii's content is not at its active edges, so it carries
a bound rather than a value — 14.1 ≤ L ≤ 70.2, which the 40 this form applies
sits inside. An RGBs source would put a card through the sliced path; the port
has nothing attached. `docs/bench-sources.md`
